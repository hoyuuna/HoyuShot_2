HOYUSHOT

A manual camera app for Android that gives you the controls of a DSLR, in your pocket. Shutter, ISO, focus, and white balance are yours to set, and the app stays out of the way.

Five exposure modes

Start with full automation and move toward complete manual control. Each mode hands you exactly the decisions you want to make.

A

Auto

The camera hardware sets ISO, shutter speed, and white balance.

P

Program

Auto exposure picks shutter and ISO. You add exposure compensation and manual focus.

S

Shutter priority

You lock the shutter speed. A software metering loop reads the live frame and sets ISO to match.

ISO

ISO priority

You lock the sensitivity. The metering loop calculates the shutter speed for you.

M

Manual

Full control of ISO, shutter speed, focus distance, and white balance. Auto exposure is off.

Exposure, focus, and color

Every value is limited to the range your lens and sensor report, so you never pick a setting the hardware cannot deliver.

Shutter speed
Presets from 1/10000 s to 30 s, filtered to your lens's exposure time range.
ISO
Presets from 50 to 12800, filtered to your sensor's sensitivity range.
Focus
Continuous autofocus, or manual focus measured in diopters from infinity to the closest focus distance.
White balance
Auto, or a Kelvin scale from 2800K to 7500K in 300K steps. Presets for daylight, cloudy, shade, fluorescent, and incandescent are also available.
Exposure compensation
A 1:1 scale along the bottom of the screen with plus and minus buttons, using your camera's full compensation range. A reset badge appears when the value is not zero.
Exposure limits
Long-press a shutter or ISO value to set a floor or ceiling for Auto and Program. When the hardware exceeds the limit, HoyuShot clamps the exposure in software and releases it when light returns.

Tools on the viewfinder

Professional aids for checking focus, exposure, and framing before you press the shutter.

Live histogram

A 256-level chart for luminance and the red, green, and blue channels, updated from the live stream.

Focus peaking

Sharp edges are highlighted in green so you can see exactly what is in focus.

Zebra warnings

Diagonal stripes mark areas that are close to clipping to white, so you can protect highlights.

Two-axis level

Shows roll and pitch against the gravity sensor. The indicator turns red and vibrates when the camera is level in all four orientations.

Sun compass

Uses GPS, the rotation sensor, and magnetic declination to show where the sun is and the light quality: backlit, frontlit, golden hour, or zenith.

Composition grid

Switch between off, a 3×3 rule-of-thirds grid, and a golden-ratio grid.

Capture and formats

Fast shutter response for everyday shooting, RAW files for editing, and metadata that records how each shot was made.

Zero shutter lag

At speeds under 350 ms the shutter releases immediately, so you can shoot in quick succession.

Burst and long exposure

Hold the shutter for continuous burst shooting with a live frame counter. Exposures of 350 ms or longer show a progress ring until they finish.

Self timer

Choose 1, 2, 5, or 10 seconds, with a large countdown and a vibration on each second.

Flash modes

Off, auto, on, or torch, working in manual, shutter priority, and ISO priority.

JPEG, RAW, and RAW+JPEG

Save DNG RAW files with the sensor's full data, or a combined RAW+JPEG pair. RAW is turned off automatically on lenses that do not support it.

Resolution and aspect ratio

Switch between 4:3, 16:9, and 1:1. The app lists up to four megapixel options that match the chosen ratio.

Noise and sharpness

Set noise reduction to off, minimum, or high quality, and sharpness to off, 0, or +1. The preview stays fast while photos get full processing.

Full EXIF and GPS

Writes ISO, shutter speed, aperture, focal length, lens make and model, and optional GPS tags to both JPEG and DNG files. Files save to DCIM/HoyuShot.

Every lens your phone has

HoyuShot looks past the default camera list to find hidden physical lenses, then labels each one by its 35 mm equivalent focal length.

Deep sensor probe

Finds manufacturer-hidden telephoto and ultrawide lenses that standard camera lists miss, and filters out depth and time-of-flight sensors.

Pinch zoom on each lens

Zoom from 1.0x to 10.0x on the current sensor without jumping to another lens. Tap the active lens button while zoomed in to return to 1x.

Zoom fallback

If a hidden lens is blocked or fails to open, HoyuShot switches to the main camera and simulates the focal length with hardware crop.

Settings carry over

When you change lens, ISO, shutter, focal length, and exposure values are adjusted to the new lens's limits automatically.

0.5MB

Light enough to leave running

HoyuShot uses about 0.5 MB of RAM while running in the background, so it does not slow down your phone or drain the battery when you switch apps.
